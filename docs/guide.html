SignSorter

Put a sign next to a chest and write what goes in it. Nearby items walk themselves into the right chest.

1 · Plain signs

A sign labels the one chest closest to it (within 2.5 m). Separate several things with commas.

No sign = inbox: items another sign names (Wood, *wood…) get taken out. It never feeds * or category chests, and personal chests without a sign are never touched. A sign that names no item ("Bob's stuff", "Don't touch") leaves its chest alone.

Arrows: < <- > -> ^ v, at the start or end of the sign.

An arrow sign reaches up to 4 m and labels only the chest it points at. ^/v skip the chest the sign is mounted on.

If left and right come out swapped in your game, turn on FlipLeftRight.

Capitals and spaces don't matter. When two chests both want an item, the more specific sign wins.

4 · Your own names: the alias file

BepInEx/config/SignSorter.aliases.txt is created the first time you launch. Edit it any time; it reloads within a couple of seconds, so you don't need to restart.

6 · Keys

Change any of these in r2modman → Config editor → local.signsorter.

F8

Sort chests within 20 m now. Reports sign entries it couldn't understand and items no sign wants.

ShiftF8

Deep sort: 60 m, repeats until settled. Also switches on auto-sort for this world.

CtrlF8

Undo the last F8 / Shift+F8, if those chests haven't changed since. Pauses auto-sort until you sort again.

F9

Empty your bag into chests whose sign names the item. Keeps equipped gear, the hotbar, and anything only a category or * would take.

Auto-sort runs every 5 seconds within 20 m once a world is switched on, and skips the work when nothing has changed. Chests anyone has open are never touched. In multiplayer, the mod only sorts chests your game controls (always true in single player, and usually true when you're the only one around).
